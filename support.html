Questions

How do I start the free trial?

Easel offers it once, right after the first-time setup. If you chose "Not now", open Easel's Settings (the gear on Today), go to Easel Plus and tap "Try Easel Plus free". The trial lasts 7 days, needs no payment details, and nothing is charged when it ends.

How do I restore my Easel Plus purchase?

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling Easel, open Settings in Easel, go to Easel Plus and tap "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Easel Plus is a one-time purchase, so it's yours for good.

What does Easel Plus include?

Scenic landscapes and Ambient art in Unwind; custom colors and seasonal moods; Nightstand mode, the morning welcome and the evening wrap-up; the full Smart Stack (Leave by, Activity, Countdown and On This Day, with smart switching); widgets, Control Center, the Action button and Siri; the Visualizer music style, extra clock styles and alternate app icons.

Free forever: your to-dos with Reminders sync, Today, the music player and turntable, the preset moods, Unwind with your photos and album art, the clock with alarm and timer, and Easel mode with Today, Weather and Quote cards.

Why does Easel ask for Calendar, Reminders, Photos, Location or Health?

Each one is optional, and Easel asks only when you first use the feature that needs it:

  • Calendar and Reminders: to show your events and keep your reminders in sync on Today.
  • Photos: to show only the photos you choose in Unwind, and On This Day.
  • Location (approximate): for the weather and the "Leave by" time to your next event.
  • Health: to show your Activity rings (read only).

If you say no, everything else keeps working. Everything stays on your iPhone; see the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn a permission on later?

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Apps, then Easel, and turn on what you'd like. For Activity rings, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Privacy, Apps, and Easel. Easel also shows a "Turn on in Settings" button wherever a feature needs a permission you turned off.

Does Easel work on iPhones that don't fold?

Yes. On a regular iPhone, Easel shows your day in a scrolling page when upright and a desk layout when turned sideways, with your to-dos, calendar, music, moods and Unwind. Features made for the fold, like the easel and book positions, appear only on iPhone Duo.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the Easel app: that removes everything Easel kept on your iPhone. Your reminders, events, photos and music stay in Apple's own apps, untouched. To stop syncing Easel's settings through iCloud, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn Easel off in the list of apps using iCloud.
