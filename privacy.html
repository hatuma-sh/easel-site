Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Easel is made by Hatim A Anwar. This page explains, in plain words, what Easel does with your information. The short version: Easel does not collect your personal data. What you keep in Easel stays on your iPhone.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in to use Easel.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We don't sell or share any data, because we don't collect any.
  • Easel has no servers of its own.

What stays on your iPhone

Easel works with these, only after you allow each one, and only on your device. Nothing from them is sent anywhere by Easel:

  • Your tasks, saved on your iPhone (and in its widgets).
  • Reminders and Calendar, to show and update your reminders and today's events. Changes go to Apple's Reminders and Calendar apps on your phone, which sync through your own accounts as they always do.
  • Photos, only the ones you choose, for the Unwind photo frame and On This Day.
  • Apple Music and the music on your iPhone, to play your playlists. Album covers for streamed songs are loaded from Apple.
  • Health, to show your Activity rings. Easel only reads them and never writes or changes anything.
  • Alarms and notifications, for the alarm, timers and reminders you set.

Easel also keeps a short diary on your iPhone to help fix problems (for example, which song was playing). It never leaves your phone.

Location

If you allow it, Easel uses your approximate location (never your precise location) for two things: the weather, through Apple's WeatherKit, and the "Leave by" travel time to your next event, through Apple's MapKit. Your location is sent to Apple for these and is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

iCloud

Your Easel settings (such as your mood, styles and layout) sync through your own iCloud account, run by Apple, so a new iPhone starts with the same look. They are stored by Apple in your iCloud, not on any server of ours.

Scenic photos from Unsplash

Scenic shows landscape photos from Unsplash. To deliver them, your iPhone connects to Unsplash, which receives standard request information such as your IP address. Easel also tells Unsplash when a photo has been shown, as Unsplash requires, without any information about you. Unsplash handles this under the Unsplash Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The free trial and Easel Plus are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.

Children

Easel is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
